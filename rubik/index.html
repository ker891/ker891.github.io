Welcome to the Rubik's Cube Solver!

This is a simple tool to help you solve all kinds of Rubik's Cube puzzles from the 2x2 to the megaminx!
The rubik's cube is a 3D combination puzzle invented in 1974 by Hungarian sculptor and professor of architecture Ernő Rubik.

The original puzzle consisted of a cube with six faces, each covered by nine stickers of one of six solid colors. nowadays, the rubik's cube has been adapted in various forms and sizes, including the 2x2, 3x3, and 4x4. Even into a dodecahedron in the megaminx and a pyramid with the pyraminx. the communities surrounding the rubik's cube has continued to develop to the point of the current 3x3 world record is 2.76 seconds and the 2x2 world record is .39 seconds!
